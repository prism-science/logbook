First CHESS beam time of the 2026-3 run cycle.

Steve M, Steve T, Neti, Mike P, Mike C, Tricia C arrived around 9 am on September 30. No significant bumping or cave optics adjustments were needed. However, in-hutch alignment was tricky because of upgrades made over the summer. Getting beam through the channel cut, CRL pass-through, slits, and cleanup aperture turned out to be pretty difficult. Our hope was to begin data collection at noon, but Mike P and Steve M finished the job just before midnight. So data collection did not begin until October 1.

One new item is the centering camera. We previously were able to automatically save camera images at certain angles. This has not been implemented yet for the new camera, so we resorted to screenshots instead. The scalebar in these images (green bar in lower left) is 100 µm.

!!! warning "multiple lattices"
    
    The well was crowded with small crystals, and evidently there are multiple in the loop, so not a useful diffuse dataset. But will be fine for checking radiation damage.

Processing with xia2: mosaicity 0.018˚, 55% indexed. B-factor decay is about 5 Å<sup>2</sup> per 180˚, or 0.1 Å<sup>2</sup> / second. So, tolerable dose limit is ~20 s at this flux. This is a bit higher than expected. Let's repeat with a fresh crystal at 0.01 second exposures.
